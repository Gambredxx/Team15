How to Activate Your Team15 Account

1

Make Payment via Airtel Money

  1. Dial *185*9# on your Airtel Money account
  2. Enter the ID: 6834846
  3. Enter the amount: UGX 15,000 (No extra charges)
  4. Enter the phone number you registered with
  5. Enter your PIN to complete the payment

Important: Use your registered phone number as reference!

2

Account Activation & Next Steps

  • Your account will be activated within 15 minutes of payment verification
  • You'll receive an SMS with your Member ID
  • Contact our support team with the link below
  • Start earning through referrals and micro-tasks
