🤑 How to Make Money on Team15 Nation

Welcome to Team15 Nation! Here’s how to start earning money step by step:

1. Register & Activate Your Account

  • Join by paying UGX 15,000 to activate your account.
  • After payment, your account will be verified manually by an admin.
  • Once approved, you’ll get full access to your earning dashboard.

2. Refer People & Earn

Invite your friends using your referral link or ID.

You earn:

  • UGX 5,000 Direct Bonus for every person who joins through you.
  • There’s no limit — the more you refer, the more you earn.

3. Earn Pairing Bonuses

Team15 uses a binary system (left + right team).

You earn:

  • UGX 3,000 for every pair of users (1 on your left + 1 on your right).
  • Even if you didn’t refer both — as long as they fall under your structure, you earn.

4. Earn Upline Bonuses

When your downlines refer others and earn, you also earn!

  • You earn a small bonus from the earnings of up to 3 levels of users below you.
  • This means your network makes money for you — passively.

5. Complete Simple Tasks Weekly

You can also earn money weekly by doing small tasks like:

  • Watching videos 📺
  • Sharing social media posts 📲
  • Answering short surveys or quizzes ✍️

Earn up to UGX 1,500 per week from tasks.

6. Withdraw Your Earnings

  • Once you reach the minimum withdrawal amount, request a p payout.
  • We pay through Mobile Money (MTN or Airtel) within 24–48 hours.

Example

If you refer 4 people:

  • 4 x UGX 5,000 = UGX 20,000 (Direct bonus)
  • If 2 go left and 2 go right → 2 pairs = UGX 6,000
  • Task earnings = up to UGX 1,500
  • Plus upline bonuses if your referrals refer others

Rules to Remember

  • Only 1 account per user.
  • Payments must be verified by admins.
  • No fake referrals or payment proofs — violators will be blocked.
  • Weekly task earnings are capped at UGX 1,500 to encourage growth through referrals.

Pro Tips

  • Focus on inviting at least 2 people (1 left, 1 right) to start pairing.
  • Help your team grow — when they earn, you earn too.
  • Do your tasks every week for extra money.
